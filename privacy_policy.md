## 1. Notice by Data Fiduciary (DPDP Act §5 / GDPR Art. 13)
CricketHub acts as the **Data Fiduciary** responsible for determining the purpose and means of processing your digital personal data.

- **Data Fiduciary Name:** CricketHub Technology Operations
- **Contact:** privacy@crickethub.app / dpo@crickethub.app
- **Purpose of Processing:** Coordinating cricket match schedules, squad availability polling, team synergy network rendering, and live ball-by-ball match scoring.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect
We adhere strictly to the principle of **Data Minimisation** (DPDP Act §6(1) / GDPR Art. 5(1)(c)). We only collect data that is strictly necessary for service delivery:

| Data Element | Purpose of Collection | Legal Basis | Retention Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mobile Phone Number** | Passwordless OTP login, session token validation, SMS match reminders | Consent / Contractual Necessity | Active account lifecycle |
| **Player Profile (Name, Emoji, Role, Team Color)** | Room identification, squad roster display, match batting/bowling lineups | Consent | Active account lifecycle |
| **Squad RSVP Availability** | Calculating attendance quorum ("Coming", "Maybe", "Can't Come") | Legitimate Purpose / Consent | Duration of active match room |
| **Match Score & Timeline Data** | Ball-by-ball score tracking, bowler wicket attribution, match analytics | Contractual Necessity | Stored in match history archive |
| **Room Chat & Announcements** | Tactical squad discussions and match announcements | Consent | Duration of room existence |
| **Web Push Subscriptions** | Delivering background match alerts, squad pings, and start notifications | Explicit User Opt-In | Revocable anytime via browser |
| **Cookie & Consent Preferences** | Remembering consent state for Essential, Analytics, and Marketing | Legal Obligation (DPDP/GDPR) | 12 months from selection |

## 4. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act §11–13 / GDPR Art. 15–21)
You possess comprehensive statutory rights over your personal data:

1. **Right to Access Information (§11):** You have the right to obtain a summary of digital personal data processed by CricketHub. (Use the **Export My Data** button in your Profile).
2. **Right to Correction and Erasure (§12):** You have the right to rectify inaccurate data or request complete deletion ("Right to be Forgotten") of your profile and data records.
3. **Right of Grievance Redressal (§13):** You have the right to register a grievance with our Data Protection Officer, with resolution guaranteed within 7 business days.
4. **Right to Nominate (§14):** You have the right to nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
5. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You can withdraw consent for cookies, push notifications, or data processing at any time without affecting past lawful processing.

## 5. Security Safeguards & Encryption
In accordance with DPDP Act §8(5) and GDPR Art. 32, CricketHub enforces industry-grade technical and organizational safeguards:
- **In-Transit Security:** TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption for all API requests and WebSocket frames.
- **At-Rest Security:** Passwordless OTPs are hashed using cryptographic salt before verification.
- **Rate Limiting & Anti-Bruteforce:** Exponential backoff delays and strict schema validation prevent credential attacks.
- **Data Isolation:** User phone numbers are masked (`+91 ••••• ••210`) when displayed to other room participants.

## 6. Cookies and Tracking Policy
- **Essential Storage (Always Active):** Used exclusively for session authentication (`authToken`) and active room state (`activeRoomId`).
- **Analytics & Performance (Opt-In):** Graphify synergy analytics and performance telemetry.
- **Marketing / Targeting (Opt-In):** Feature notifications and match discovery.

You can modify your cookie choices at any time using the persistent floating **Cookie Settings 🍪** button.
