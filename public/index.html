iPhone Push Setup

Apple requires installing to your iPhone Home Screen via Safari for background alerts.

1
Open this app link in Safari (Apple blocks background push in Chrome on iOS).
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with arrow ⬆️).
3
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ➕, then tap "Add".
4
Open CricketHub from your iPhone Home Screen & tap "Enable 🔔".
